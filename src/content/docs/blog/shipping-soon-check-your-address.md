Hi Friends,

Big milestone: every PwrTool 500 for this campaign is boxed, labeled, and waiting at Mouser’s warehouse in Texas as of this week. From there, the warehouse will ship them out to each of you.

One thing we need from you right now: please double check that your shipping address is current in your [Crowd Supply account](https://www.crowdsupply.com/account). You can contact [Crowd Supply Support](https://www.crowdsupply.com/contact) to update your address. Once your order ships, changing the address ranges from difficult to impossible, so a minute now saves a lot of headache later.

On timing: Mouser is working through a serious receiving backlog (they just doubled the size of their warehouse and the new building is already filling with pallets), so the current turnaround from their dock to your door is running about four weeks. Not the instant gratification we’d love to give you, but the units are physically there and the clock is running.

Thank you for backing us and for your patience through this build. We can’t wait to see PwrTool 500s showing up in your Home Assistant dashboards.
